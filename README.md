# Kill Chain Sparring — Red vs Blue

A browser-based cybersecurity training game built around the seven-stage cyber kill chain and MITRE ATT&CK.

**[Play it here](https://theotherenya.github.io/kill-chain-game/)** — no login or install needed.

## How it works

Each round is one stage of the kill chain, from Reconnaissance through Actions on Objectives. Red Team picks an attack technique (referencing real MITRE ATT&CK technique IDs), Blue Team picks the control meant to counter it. Guess right as Blue, and the chain breaks — game over, defenders win. Let every stage through unblocked, and Red achieves full compromise.

Three ways to play:
- **Solo vs AI** — take either Red or Blue, with three AI difficulty levels
- **Two players, one device** — pass-and-play, no AI involved
- **Practice mode** — play through all seven stages regardless of outcome, useful for drilling every stage rather than stopping at the first win

## Why

Built as a simple, flowchart-style way for beginners and more experienced folks alike to get a feel for kill chain thinking and common ATT&CK mitigations, without needing a full simulated environment.

## Notes

Technique-to-mitigation mappings are simplified for gameplay and aren't exhaustive security guidance — treat this as a teaching aid, not a reference architecture.
